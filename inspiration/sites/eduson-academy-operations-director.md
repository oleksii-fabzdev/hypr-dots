## Why it's here

Long-form paid-course landing page. Overall execution is solid — clean and
confident rather than loud, which is rare for this category. Three things stood
out enough to want them later.

## What I liked

### Price typography
The font used for prices/numbers. It carries the pricing block on its own
without needing badges, strikethroughs, or "SALE" chrome around it — the number
*is* the visual. Worth pulling the exact `font-family` and the size/weight jump
between the price and the surrounding body text next time I'm on the page.

### Buttons
The button treatment across the page — shape, weight, and how they sit against
the background. Consistent enough that the CTA reads as the same object
everywhere it appears, which is what makes a long scrolling page feel like one
document instead of a stack of sections.

### Image layout
How the photos/screenshots are arranged. The grid does the pacing for the page:
it breaks up long text stretches without turning into a gallery you have to
stop and look at.
